# Raw evidence and freshness/lookback contracts

Status: boundaries and fail-closed evaluators are implemented for synthetic fixtures. Provider-specific profiles remain disabled/unverified until rights and upstream technical evidence are authoritative.

## Capture and retention boundary map

| Stage | Exact captured object/boundary | Retention contract |
|---|---|---|
| Raw provider response | Exact response-body octets at one declared transport stage; capture profile enumerates included/excluded bytes, e.g. body after decompression and before character decoding. Credentials, headers and transport framing are excluded unless a separately approved profile explicitly says otherwise. | Approved raw-provider-body class: 30 days, only where terms permit. RawProfile is bound to the exact byte representation. |
| Parsed provider record | One provider row after syntax parsing, with original source timestamp spelling/numeric lexemes, page ID, row ordinal and raw-body reference. Parsing does not turn it into a normalized candle. | No duration is inferred; provider retention terms and a cited duration are required. |
| Normalized observation | A separately versioned XAUUSD identity/timeframe with exact decimal OHLCV, source timestamp spelling, normalized aware UTC instant, source/retrieval IDs and validation status. | No duration is inferred; provider rights for normalized retention and cited duration are required. |
| Rejected record | Original retrieval/page/row ordinal, rejection code and only the rejected bytes/text permitted by terms. Secrets are excluded; a denial becomes metadata-only. | Approved rejected-row class: 90 days, subject to provider rights for retained content. |
| Duplicate occurrence | A separate occurrence ID retaining page and row ordinal and linking to accepted revision where applicable. Identical content at another ordinal remains a separate occurrence. | Approved duplicate-occurrence class: 90 days, subject to provider rights. |
| Derived analysis input | Input-manifest identity/digest and selected immutable revision set; no alternate normalized copy is implied. | Provider rights cover derived/internal use and any retained input; duration remains provider-term-defined unless separately approved. |
| AI prompt / response | Exact prompt/response byte representation at the configured adapter boundary, redacted of credentials and linked to request, model, manifest and retention metadata. | Approved AI prompt/response class: 30 days; external AI processing permission is separately required. No external call is made by these contracts. |

`CaptureProfile` maps each stage to separate permission gates and attribution evidence. Affirmative permission requires an authoritative reference. Raw byte profiles must match the explicit byte boundary. Provider-defined retention requires a cited duration source. Unknown or denied permissions, missing attribution, or missing duration keep the profile disabled. The synthetic tests contain fixture-only permissions; they are not provider evidence. Twelve Data and SiftingIO remain UNVERIFIED.

## Freshness/lookback v1

`FreshnessPolicyV1` separates three clocks: represented market-event time (data age at evaluation), provider publication time, and retrieval completion time. It measures publication delay from market-event time to provider publication and retrieval delay from publication to completed retrieval. Each timeframe may carry independently evidenced limits for event age, publication delay, retrieval delay and fallback lookback. Missing limits yield explicit unavailable reasons; there are no production numerical defaults. A whole retrieval must be complete and source permission must be ELIGIBLE. Historical fallback additionally requires an explicit policy switch and a bounded timeframe lookback. Candidates with conflicting content at the same latest event time are unavailable.

Market OPEN is an explicit context. CLOSED/HOLIDAY states require a calendar evidence reference; the evaluator does not invent market hours or holiday boundaries and applies no implicit closure grace. It still evaluates the configured age threshold. Stale, incomplete, denied/unverified, future-timestamp, delayed-publication, unbounded fallback, invalidated-evidence and conflicting-retrieval cases return unavailable reasons. Historical fallback is permitted only by a versioned explicit switch and evidenced lookback bound; it does not bypass the normal event-age, completeness, permission or conflict checks. Missing timeframe limits and unverified calendar evidence remain unavailable. Synthetic tests exercise stale event age, publication and retrieval delays, confirmed closure/holiday with and without calendar evidence, fallback, conflicting latest revisions, invalidation, missing timeframe thresholds and unsupported H4. H4 is always unavailable under this implementation because H4-03 remains deferred.

Synthetic threshold values in `test_freshness_policy.py` are only fixtures and do not express approved production settings. Required external/product evidence still includes each source's publication/update cadence, correction/finality behavior, timestamp semantics, permitted delay/freshness expectations, market calendar coverage and permissible historical lookback. No fallback is enabled in any production configuration.
