# Deterministic XAUUSD analysis contract

The offline engine is `xau.deterministic.research` version `1` in `backend/app/analysis/engine.py`. It accepts a validated `EvidencePacket`; it does not call providers, storage, AI, or execution systems. Every derived record carries its method/version string and source candle references. Synthetic fixtures are labeled synthetic and are not market observations.

## Input gate

`EvidenceCandle` requires exact decimal OHLC, `low <= open/close <= high`, aware timestamps normalized to UTC, a candle duration exactly matching its declared timeframe, and an opening aligned to that UTC timeframe boundary. The engine requires strictly increasing candle openings; duplicates are flagged and excluded, while reversed order is marked invalid and excluded. Forming candles do not enter calculations. Missing expected bucket openings are retained on the timeframe with `missing_candles`; calculations are marked insufficient. Evidence from more than one source series is never blended within a timeframe. Pipeline lineage carries the selected retrieval ID and a revision ID derived from retrieval, opening, and source-record hash.

| Calculation | Parameters and version | Inputs | Unavailable or limited when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Swing highs/lows | Strict local extreme, left 2 / right 2 closed candles by default; `swing_strict_extreme_left_N_right_N_v1` | Complete ordered candles; all neighbors must be strictly lower/higher | Ties do not form a swing; fewer than `left + right + 1` candles |
| Market structure, BOS/CHoCH | Close crosses the latest confirmed swing; first break is BOS, later opposite break CHoCH; `structure_confirmed_close_break_v1` | Confirmed swings and closed candles | Requires a prior confirmed swing and close crossing; wick-only breaks are ignored |
| Support/resistance | Confirmed swing lows/highs clustered by absolute price distance `0.10` USD/troy ounce; cluster mean; `confirmed_swing_{support,resistance}_cluster_abs_tolerance_T_v1` | Confirmed swing points | No levels until a swing exists; tolerance is a research default, not a calibrated production setting |
| Liquidity levels | Confirmed swing highs/lows plus equal-swing pairs within absolute `0.10`; `confirmed_swing_liquidity_reference_v1`, `equal_swing_levels_abs_tolerance_T_v1` | Confirmed swings | These are potential liquidity references, not evidence of resting orders |
| Liquidity sweeps | Wick crosses a confirmed level and candle closes back through it; `sweep_wick_through_close_back_v1` | Confirmed swing, closed candle | No claim on wick-only touches without close rejection |
| Fair Value Gaps | Three-candle strict non-overlap; later overlap is partial mitigation, full traversal is mitigated; `fvg_three_candle_nonoverlap_v1` | Ordered closed OHLC candles | No gap when boundaries touch/overlap; observations end at retrieval-window end |
| Order Blocks | Last opposing candle before BOS/CHoCH; break range at least `1.5 * ATR(14)`; later close beyond zone invalidates, later overlap mitigates; `last_opposing_candle_close_break_displacement_T_atr_v1` | Structure event and ATR-14 | Unavailable without ATR warm-up, opposing candle, close break, or displacement; mitigation is only observed through available history |
| EMA trend | EMA 20/50/200, SMA-seeded then alpha `2/(n+1)`; `ema_sma_seed_alpha_2_over_n_plus_1_v1`, alignment `ema_20_50_200_ordering_v1` | Closed closes | Each EMA requires its period of candles; non-strict ordering is mixed |
| VWAP | HLC3 price weighted by all volume in the selected retrieval window; `vwap_hlc3_retrieval_window_exchange_or_contract_volume_v1` | Every candle must have nonnegative exchange-traded or contract volume and total volume > 0 | Tick counts, unspecified, missing, or mixed volume make VWAP unavailable. Window is not a session; no session boundary is inferred |
| ATR and volatility | Wilder ATR(14), first TR is high-low; later TR includes prior-close gaps; normalized volatility is ATR(14)/latest close * 100; `atr_true_range_wilder_sma_seed_v1`, `atr14_over_close_percent_v1` | Closed OHLC; 14 candles | ATR unavailable before 14 candles; no high/low volatility category is asserted |
| RSI and momentum | Wilder RSI(14), zero-loss => 100 and flat => 50; `rsi_wilder_sma_seed_zero_loss_100_flat_50_v1`. Ten-interval close rate-of-change percent; `close_rate_of_change_percent_v1` | Closed closes | RSI needs 15 candles; momentum needs 11; unavailable states are explicit |
| Multi-timeframe alignment | Compare each timeframe's EMA 20/50/200 ordering; `multitimeframe_ema20_50_200_alignment_v1` | At least two independently available, verified timeframes | Fewer than two produces no alignment; missing/insufficient timeframes make the result partial and insufficient. It is not a trade signal |

Default `AnalysisParameters` are frozen per run: swing left/right 2, equal-level tolerance `0.10`, order-block displacement 1.5 ATR, and configured IANA wall-clock session definitions. The Qatar display context uses `Asia/Qatar`. Session definitions remain context labels and do not define candle/session boundaries.

The pipeline selects candles from only the latest whole completed retrieval attempt for each interval. A newer pending, failed, or incomplete attempt yields no usable candles and does not fall back to an older retrieval. Missing bucket openings are enumerated within the selected requested range; gaps are never filled. H4 is explicitly unsupported in this pipeline while H4-03 is deferred. The current pipeline contract has no 1-minute timeframe; 1M remains unavailable here.

No production source is eligible or activated by these calculations. A synthetically complete page chain is not authoritative provider completeness, and analysis against synthetic data is never presented as live market data.
